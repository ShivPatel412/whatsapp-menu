# What “free” means here

Reviewed 18 November 2026. Check the linked provider pages before publishing
your tutorial or enabling real traffic.

| Component | Free path | What can cost money |
|---|---|---|
| This repository | Template, guide and tests under MIT | Optional custom work outside this repo |
| n8n | Self-hosted Community edition | Hosting, domain, maintenance, paid n8n plans/features |
| Menu-only edition | No model/API key needed | WhatsApp delivery and hosting |
| Gemini | Eligible model/project free tier | Model availability, quotas and paid usage |
| Meta WhatsApp | Monthly service-message allowance | Delivered messages beyond allowances; other categories |
| Coexistence provider | VGraple advertises a free plan | Plan limits, paid features and Meta delivery charges |

Meta currently states **1,000 free delivered service messages per phone number per
month**, with charges from the 1,001st; the tier does not roll over. Non-template
replies still require an open 24-hour customer-service window. Check the current
[Meta pricing](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing/)
and your account's rates. A conversation containing multiple replies can consume
multiple messages. Do not call this “unlimited free WhatsApp.”

Google's free tier is model/project limited and can use content to improve its
products. Paid services have different data terms. Check
[Gemini pricing](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/pricing) and
[terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms) before handling customer information.

VGraple's free plan is a provider option, not a dependency of this workflow.
Check its [current pricing](https://vgraple.co.in/pricing), limits and eligibility.
The AI in this repository runs in n8n; it is not VGraple's paid “bring your own
AI key” feature.

n8n's [Community edition](https://docs.n8n.io/hosting/community-edition-features/)
has its own license. This repo does not redistribute or relicense n8n.

**Safe Reel claim:** “Free WhatsApp menu template with a full setup guide.”
**Avoid:** “Free forever, unlimited AI replies, no hosting or Meta charges.”
