## Hook options

1. **Recommended: cold-open demo**
   - Spoken: “Watch my WhatsApp collect a quote request.”
   - On screen: “HI → QUOTE REQUEST”
   - First frame: already typing HI, with the response immediately visible.

2. Reusable artifact
   - Spoken: “Use this WhatsApp menu for your own business.”
   - On screen: “YOUR WHATSAPP MENU TEMPLATE”

3. Before/after
   - Spoken: “This used to be a blank chat. Now it guides the enquiry.”
   - On screen: “CHAT → GUIDED ENQUIRY”
   - Do not imply a measured time saving or a result you have not measured.

Hook 1 is the best fit: it shows the actual behavior before explaining the tools.

## What the Reel does versus the full setup

The Reel demonstrates the outcome and sends viewers to the repository.
Use a separate longer tutorial for account onboarding; don’t squeeze secret/token
screens into a 50-second demo.

### Suggested 8–12 minute tutorial chapters

1. Result first: menu, quote flow, bold questions, About Us.
2. Free template versus running costs; choose menu-only or Gemini.
3. Download/import JSON into n8n; keep inactive.
4. Create the five-column conversation table and edit PROFILE.
5. Configure Meta test number and credentials privately.
6. Verify production webhook and Messaging account subscription.
7. Optional Gemini key/model and data-use explanation.
8. Test from another number; inspect accepted/delivered status.
9. Existing-number coexistence and linked-device caveats.
10. Publish only after tests, with a final cost/permission recap.
